Story

I work under the name HAT. My work sits between the sketch and the real car: a kit you can bolt on, a wheel you can buy, a visual a team can sign off.

In 2026 I designed No Noise Club: the identity, the SEED bolt-on body kit for the VinFast VF7, the wheels and the “Silence is the Flex” shirts. The kit is on sale.

At B-Factory Vietnam I design body kits that reach production. The Limo Green kit is on sale, and from my VF3 concept the hood is now on sale.

Before that I was Visualization Lead at VinFast. My team shipped 50+ presentation visuals in seven months, across the Green lineup and VF6 to VF9.
