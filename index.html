/ 01 — Hero build

VinFast VF7 Widebody

A VF7 kit on my own brief, with no client. The arches go as wide as the surfaces allow, cut into the stock body, with skirts, splitter, ducktail and rear wing. It sits beside SEED, the bolt-on NNC kit for the same car that is on sale now, and showed which choices on that kit were engineering and which were habit. It also runs in Assetto Corsa as a drivable car.

/ 02 — CMF

One body, 10 finishes

Every livery is printed onto a body UV atlas built for the car, so lines run across panel gaps without seams and text reads the right way on both sides. Some come from the car's Assetto Corsa build; others, like the glass and frosted finishes, were made for this site and run as live shaders.

[ Studio ]

Designs that end up on real cars. Body kits, wheels and liveries, made for production.

HAT is the practice of Huy Anh Tran, automotive concept designer in Ho Chi Minh City. A strong stance, controlled surfaces, and light that shows the form instead of hiding it. Blender and CG for the form, real-time to test it, AI to explore options and finish faster. AI images are labelled.

Capabilities

  1. 01

    Body kits

    Full kits from sketch to mould draft and fitment check. On sale: the NNC SEED kit for the VF7 and the B-Factory Limo Green kit.

  2. 02

    Concept cars

    From first sketch to a car whose stance and intent read in one look, before anything gets built.

  3. 03

    CMF & liveries

    Colour, material and finish sets and wraps, designed and applied to real cars.

  4. 04

    Visualization

    Presentation visuals, campaign images, film and drivable real-time cars. 50+ visuals in seven months at VinFast.

  5. 05

    Identity

    Brand, body kit and apparel as one visual system, as for No Noise Club.
